Undercut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026.

Undercut ("we", "us") provides an automated eBay repricing service. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account data: the email address you use to sign up.
  • eBay data (only with your consent via eBay OAuth): your active listings, prices, SKUs, and quantities, used to reprice on your behalf. We never receive or store your eBay password.

How we use it

  • To import your listings and automatically adjust prices according to the rules you set — undercut competitors, never below your floor.
  • To show you price-change history and competitor pricing.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers required to operate the service (eBay's APIs, our hosting provider, and our AI provider) and as required by law.

Your eBay authorization

You can revoke Undercut's access at any time from eBay: Account Settings → Sign in and security → Third-party app access.

Data retention & deletion

We retain your data while your account is active. Email privacy@undercut.app to request deletion of your account and data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@undercut.app.
